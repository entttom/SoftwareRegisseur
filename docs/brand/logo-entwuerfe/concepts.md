# Software Regisseur — logo concepts, round two

## 1. Cue Frame

Four square viewfinder corners and a custom geometric R share a measured grid. The R is a solid, cleanly cut shape with clear space around it; together the frame and initial suggest a director choosing and holding a shot.

**Palette:** Oxblood `#7C302D` on warm paper `#F2EBDD`.

**Menu bar:** `concept-1-menubar.svg` is redrawn for a 16 × 16 px template image with a 1.5 px frame and a compact filled R. It uses black on transparency so macOS can apply the menu bar foreground color.

## 2. Branch Script

A two-ended continuous route draws an R: one point starts the stem, the shoulder turns into a bowl, and the leg resolves the letter. The two endpoints hint at a directed handoff between a person and an agent while keeping the Git reference subordinate to the Regisseur initial.

**Palette:** Deep pine `#243B32` on pale sage `#D9E3D4`.

**Menu bar:** Keep the thick R route and its two endpoint discs. Its open counter and distinct diagonal leg preserve the letter at 16 px.

## 3. Cue for Three

A squared viewfinder cue opens toward three identical marks in a disciplined vertical row. The larger frame stands for the director; the repeated marks stand for a small cast of coding agents receiving the same clear direction.

**Palette:** Dark umber `#4D3828` on warm sand `#E9D8B8`.

**Menu bar:** The open frame and three compact bars reduce to a strong left-to-right cue with clear spacing at 16 px.

## 4. Regisseur R

A single, broad geometric R uses a squared stem and bowl with a decisive diagonal leg. It is a typographic signature rather than a framed cue, and its heavier silhouette distinguishes it from the finer, open corners of Concept 1.

**Palette:** Soft parchment `#F0E8D9` on deep slate-green `#263B3A`.

**Menu bar:** Use the simplified single-color R silhouette. Its broad strokes and open counter keep the initial distinct at 16 px.

## Recommendation

**Recommend Concept 1, Cue Frame.** The frame gives the product a clear directing idea, while the custom R makes it specific to Regisseur. Its measured grid and dedicated menu bar drawing make it the strongest system across app icon and macOS use.
